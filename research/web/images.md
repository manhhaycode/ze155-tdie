Notes for the designer/builder
- The ZE UT large-machine look (web-01..05): round barrel sections joined by bolted flange rings (not C-clamps), heater shells, square junction boxes under each barrel, a cooling manifold with one valve set per zone along the base frame, blue gearbox and lube unit, white base frame. Newer KM machines (web-06, 07, 16) put hinged stainless hoods with yellow warning labels over the barrels and use white plus KM blue.
- web-07 and web-08 show how KM puts a slot die on a ZE: melt pump with vertical drive motor, short adapter, die held on its own wheeled cart or frame.
- Catalogue images (pdf analyst) remain the primary source for the ZE UT silhouette; these photos give the details.
